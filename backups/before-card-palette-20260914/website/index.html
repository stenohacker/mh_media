Reporter to reporter

Hi, fellow reporter.

I built these tools because I know how much invisible work happens before the first word is spoken and after the last page is proofed. I wanted AI that already understands our workflow—not another generic tool we have to teach from scratch.

Whether you’re preparing a dictionary, untangling difficult testimony, punctuating, proofreading, or describing exhibits, each tool is designed to give you clear answers in the right format.

What matters to me: the tools verify information when they can, flag uncertainty instead of guessing, and tell you when something deserves a human review.

You don’t need any AI experience. My hope is simple: less repetitive work, fewer frustrating detours, and more confidence in every transcript you deliver.

— Tamara
